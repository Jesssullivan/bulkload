- Path: `/Volumes/TinylandState/tinyland-state/tummycrypt-litter-20260921/.bulkload-bench-real-ee15653/corpus`.

  in r23-2026-09-18, `04a750df...` in m0/w3 (see "Identity" below for why these differ).

## Replacement: R23 corpus v1

`crates/bulkload-bench/scripts/r23_corpus.py` (`just bench-r23-corpus`)
generates the corpus deterministically. Every byte comes from SHAKE-256 in
counter mode with the fixed seed `bulkload-r23-corpus-v1`; it reads no live
files. The shape follows the old workload:
- 23 regular files, 239,819,837 bytes (old: 242,605,606).
- Sizes: four large files (88, 40, 32 and 16 MiB), eight medium files
  (2–8 MiB) and ten small ones (1 B – 1 MiB).
- Compressibility is mixed: incompressible random, about 4 bits/byte text,
  and sparse files that alternate zero and random 64 KiB runs.
- Two duplicates are planted: `copies/blob-b-copy.bin` is a whole-file copy
  of `big/blob-b.bin` (16 MiB), and `medium/m04-prefix.bin` starts with
  blob-b's first 3 MiB. That gives at most 19 MiB (8.3 %) of cross-file
  duplicate bytes, so dedup loss is measurable.
- Files are 0644, directories 0755, and every mtime is 2026-10-02T00:00:00Z.

**Content identity:** `f4a7619f7b88f2e0e1eeadb5995c79a809eafa8b4a8cf3f82d1ff6f29b5b07c7`.
It is the BLAKE3 of the committed manifest
(`crates/bulkload-bench/scripts/r23_corpus.manifest.tsv`): one
`path\tsize\tblake3` line per regular file, sorted by path bytes.

## Identity: content versus the bench's stat identity

The bench's `corpus_identity` hashes postcard-encoded `RowSchema` rows,
which include `dev`, `ino`, `mtime_ns`, `ctime_ns` and `nlink`. It
identifies one copy on one filesystem, not the content. That is why the lost
corpus showed `9b52260a...` on 09-18 and `04a750df...` on 09-23: the row
schema or the stat fields changed between those runs. A stat identity cannot
be committed or reproduced, so the committed identity covers paths, sizes
and content only. `r23_corpus.py verify` checks the remaining fields that
the bench's `same_payload` compares (kind, mode 0644/0755, no extra
entries). The bench's `sealed_corpus_blake3` is still printed and recorded
for every copy, as informational.

- `diff -r` of the two sting copies reports them byte-identical. The neo
  copy was generated by the same script at `c82c711` (b3sum 1.8.5 from
  nixpkgs) and verifies to the same content identity. That is three
  generations on two hosts, two operating systems and three filesystems.
- The bench check is one informational native-only run of the B binary
  (`643b58db33dc`, `--only native --reps 1 --informational`), run outside
  any gated timing. The bench verified the fixture against the sealed corpus
  with full BLAKE3 (`same_payload`). Its 27 rows are 23 files and 4
  directories. The stat identities differ, as expected.
- Each sealed copy holds `corpus/`, `MANIFEST.tsv` and a `README.md` that
  marks it PROTECTED under the R23 gate.
- Both archived copies were then made read-only with `r23_corpus.py seal`
  at `40b820e` (files 0444, directories 0555, including the copy root), and
  both still verify to `f4a7619f...` (R-N71 round 1, item 8). `verify`
  accepts 0644/0755 or 0444/0555 and rejects extra files and directories.
- The bench mutates its private fixture for the 1 % delta, and that fixture
  copies the source's modes, so the bench cannot read a read-only tree
  directly. `r23_ab.py` therefore copies the sealed corpus once into its work
  root with 0644/0755 modes and verifies that copy before the builds. Every
  rep must report the same `sealed_corpus_blake3` for that copy. After the
  last rep, both the copy and the sealed corpus are verified again.

## Baseline break

- The rows in `r23-2026-09-18.md`, `m0-2026-09-23.md` and `w3-2026-09-23.md`
  were measured on the lost corpus (`9b52260a...`/`04a750df...`). New samples
  use corpus v1 (`f4a7619f...`). Absolute times are **not comparable** across
  that break: the shape is close, but the content, compressibility and
  duplicate structure differ.
- Within one harness session (`r23_ab.py`), A, B, v4 and rclone all run on
  the same corpus copy, so the A/B/rclone comparisons and medians stay
  internally comparable. Gate (a) is decided from one such session: the
  order is B/A/B/A/B, and B passes R23 only if every B rep's bench verdict
  passes (OI-1002-Q30, AGENTS.md R23 amendment). A is informational.
- The design target (median < 1.5 s on the TinylandState corpus) is now read
  against corpus v1 on TinylandState.
