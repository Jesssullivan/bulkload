Where the reads and writes go now:

- Reads are the source files (P) plus 15,842,692 B of destination-local
  reuse: chunks repeated across the corpus, re-read from outputs written
  earlier in the session and re-verified.
- Writes are the outputs (P) plus the source pack (U). The pack stays for
  R25 source-side resume without re-reading. W4 removes it (R-N58).
- BLAKE3 on the source is 2 × P: a per-chunk hash plus the whole-file
  digest the `Manifest` frame still carries (wire v4), both accumulated in one read. On
  the destination it is 1 × P: each received chunk is hashed once (the
  load-bearing check, U bytes), plus the P − U reused bytes.

## Why the median is still above 1.5 s

The USB volume explains only part of the remaining time. Every initial sample
writes P + U = 469.4 MB to TinylandState, and at M0's measured USB write rate
of 250 to 360 MB/s that alone takes 1.3 to 1.9 s. But the recorded barrier
wait (2.18 to 2.95 s per sample) is mostly serial: one committer thread per
store issues one `F_BARRIERFSYNC` per file, each waiting for that file's data
to reach the device before the next begins (PR #59 review). The device queue
is therefore not kept full, and "device-bound" overstates the case. Issuing
the per-file barriers in parallel is a W4 lever, alongside removing the
source pack. `--durability=strict` gave an informational
median of 2,298 ms against 2,133 and 2,719 ms for group mode, and M0 had
already shown flush kind barely matters on this device. Getting under 1.5 s
on TinylandState needs fewer device bytes, which means W4's removal of the
source pack. Delta did not regress: the native median was 117 ms against
368 ms for rclone.

## R25 checks

- Warm resume: transferred 0 and source read 0 in every sample.
- Interrupted resume: transferred 0 and source read 0, `r25_interrupted_zero=true`.
- Delta: the transfer went from 2,444,912 B to 3,280,458 B. The bench removes
  the mutated destination files before the delta phase. The destination no
  longer keeps a byte pack, so the unchanged chunks of those deleted files
  are sent again. This is not an R-N58 violation: the destination no
  longer holds those bytes. Source bytes read are unchanged (3,280,458 B).
- The captures and outputs identity matches hold. Unit tests cover them:
  `interrupted_transport_resumes_completed_captures_without_source_reads`,
  and in `tests/w3_engine.rs` the warm, rebuilt and adopted cases.
