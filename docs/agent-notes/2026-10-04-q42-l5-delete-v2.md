fail only this file, while P66 stays green. The first drops `--shallow`
from `thin_pack` and fails 5 cases. The second has `present()` keep only
commit haves and fails 2 cases. The plan's P46 disposition and P66 row now
record the split, the three cases not carried (`segmented-64k`,
