## The failure

Once on honey (`~/git-build/logs/beside-adopt-converge-testall-20c8981.log`),
`git_carry::source_inert_tests::p34_a_capture_leaves_the_source_lstat_census_unchanged`
panicked at `source_inert_tests.rs:296:50` with `CaptureDrift {
ObjectStoreRewritten "objects/pack" }` (minimal shape: `packed: true`,
one stash, a staged revert, two untracked files). `export_repository_inner`
returns that only when one of the export's Git children refused
(`GIT_CHILD_FAILED` or `GIT_INVENTORY_MALFORMED`) and the source's pack
listing differed from the one read before the pass. It discarded both
the refusal and the listings (it kept a hash), so the log could not say
which child failed or what moved.

## What changed

- `Exported::ObjectStoreRewritten { drift, rewrite }`: `rewrite` is a
  `StoreRewrite` holding the absorbed refusal and every `pack` entry that
  appeared, vanished or was replaced, with inode and size before and
  after, per store.
- estate-capture's `deferred-with-drift` receipt names them in its reason:
  `rewrite_cause=<refusal> rewrite_changed=<n>` (help text updated).
- `pack_listing` keeps the entries. An entry removed between `readdir` and
  its stat is still absent (that is the rewrite). Any other stat failure
  now refuses with its errno: before, it was recorded as `(0, u64::MAX)`,
  so a transient stat failure after a child's failure read as a rewrite
  and turned that failure into drift custody.
- p34's panic prints the rewrite.

## Does the capture write the source?

No, as far as this lane could see. p34 (its six generated shapes, each a
capture and two incremental passes) ran once under `strace -f` (`execve`,
`chdir`, `clone*`, every open with a write flag, `rename*`, `unlink*`,
`link*`, `mkdir*`, `utimensat`, `truncate`, `chmod`), and every write-class
call on a path under a p34 source was attributed to the process that made
it (each pid's argv and cwd tracked; the analyzer was a scratch script,
not kept). 1876 Git processes ran. Every write under a source's `.git`
came from the fixture's own commands (`init`, `add`, `commit`, `repack`
and the `pack-objects` it runs, `stash` and the `reset`, `update-index`
and `clean` it runs, all carrying the fixture's `-c user.name=Bulkload
test`), or from the test process itself: its `utimensat` backdating of the
objects, its worktree writes and its cleanup. None came from any capture
child (`rev-parse`, `for-each-ref`, `ls-files`, `hash-object`, `mktree`,
`fast-import`, `bundle`, `fetch`, `index-pack`, `cat-file`, `update-ref`,
`reflog`, ...), so this is not an S2 defect, and no S2 fix (or extra
byte-identity test) was made. p34's own `lstat` census (mode, size, mtime,
ctime, inode, link count of every source node, `.git` included) already
fails on any write, a content change included, since a write moves ctime.

What moved `objects/pack` in the one failure is still not established.
The two ways it could happen without a real rewrite were a stat failure
read as an absent entry (fixed here) and a write by bulkload (not seen).
The next occurrence names the failing child and the moved entries.
