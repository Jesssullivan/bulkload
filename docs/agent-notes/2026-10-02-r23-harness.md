## Host and corpus decision

- **Gate (a) is single-host (R-N134), on neo.** The gate in `docs/design.md`
  is defined on the TinylandState corpus, and the device (USB APFS flush
  cost) is part of what is measured. A sting copy on `/srv/data` would
  measure a different filesystem and device, and would not be comparable to
  m0/w3/r23 numbers.
- sting cannot produce a gated sample anyway. The bench's R-N81 preflight
  reads `pmset`, so on Linux it reports `power=unknown` and refuses. sting's
  load1 was 17.5 at 13:49 local, too.
- **Blocker found:** the corpus of record
  (`/Volumes/TinylandState/tinyland-state/tummycrypt-litter-20260921/.bulkload-bench-real-ee15653/corpus`,
  23 files, 242,605,606 bytes) is **missing on neo**. The `bulkload-m0-*`
  and `bulkload-w3-*` log dirs are gone from TinylandState as well. No copy
  was found on sting (searched `/srv/data`, `/srv/fast-local/jess`, `~`
  to depth 7). The harness refuses any corpus whose shape is not 23 / 242,605,606
  unless `--expect-files/--expect-bytes` are given. Re-sealing a
  replacement corpus is an operator decision.

## Precondition logic

Gated mode refuses to start unless: Darwin; `--coordinator-quiet` (R-N91,
recorded); corpus shape matches; the work root is new. After the builds it
waits up to `--settle-seconds` (900) for AC power and load1 < 2.5. It checks
before every rep. After every rep it checks for AC power and for
`gated=true` on every bench row. The bench itself checks before every arm. Any
failure aborts the sample: exit 3, evidence written with `status=aborted`.
The page cache is never dropped. The bench's full BLAKE3 source verify before
every arm makes every timed arm source-hot by construction. Residency is
logged before every rep.
