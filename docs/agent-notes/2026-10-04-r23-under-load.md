## What the harness does under load

- `crates/bulkload-bench/scripts/r23_ab.py --under-load` runs sealed corpus
  v1 with the gated mode's Darwin, corpus, verify and seal checks, and
  passes `--informational` to the bench.
- Only the load gate is lifted. AC power is required once before the first
  rep (waiting up to `--settle-seconds`), before every rep, right after
  every rep, and on every bench sample row, refused informational reps
  included. `--informational` stops the bench refusing an arm on battery,
  so the harness checks the rows itself.
- The post-rep load wait is skipped; load1 is recorded, not gated.
- `--coordinator-quiet` is not required; when given it is recorded as
  acknowledged only (`coordinator_quiet_meaning` in the JSON).
- The rollup is never a pass count: it lists B's bench statuses (for example
  `informational x3`) and the median of the bench's informational
  native-vs-rclone initial and delta medians. The evidence adds a per-rep
  bench medians table and cites `RULINGS_UNDER_LOAD` (OI-1003-Q39,
  OI-1003-Q50; no R-N81/R-N91).
- Evidence goes to `docs/evidence/r23-underload-<date>-<HHMM>Z.md`; an
  `--evidence` name without `underload` is refused.
