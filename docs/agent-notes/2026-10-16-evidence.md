## Correction to the series summary as relayed

The relayed reading said fold-on is "~65 ms faster in every paired
round". The data show fold on faster in all five paired rounds, but by
9.3 to 79.9 ms; 65.3 ms is the gap between the medians of the round
medians. The A control (the same binary in both arms) was also slower in
every fold-off run, by 12.3 to 50.1 ms, and fold off always ran second,
so the series confounds the fold with order and drift. The receive tail
accounts for about 27.6 ms. The "1 % delta worse than rclone in 9 of 10
rounds" reading holds (round 2 fold on is the exception, 0.963x).
