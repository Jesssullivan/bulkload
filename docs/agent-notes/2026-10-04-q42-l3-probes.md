## Results in short

All figures are informational, sting, ungated. load1 was 24 to 87 from
other lanes, so CPU, not wall, is the comparison.

- **Header against the cap.**
  - `carry-119761`: header 20,745,391 B, +3,968,175 over 16,777,216.
    `git-export` refuses `GIT_INVENTORY_MALFORMED`, exit 1. `estate-capture`
    refuses the item with the same code, and the verb exits
    `CONTRACT_SELF_INCONSISTENT`. No panic, no IO.
  - `compact-119761`: 8,761,559 B on the first pass and 8,817,287 B
    chained, both ok.
  - A canonical carry ref line is 138 B plus its suffix, so a carry
    inventory of 119,761 refs never fits. The blahaj-like shape reaches the
    cap at about 96,855 refs.
- **Cap window (grouped items).** At 96,849 refs the base header is
  16,754,957 B (ok). Both items' recorded headers are 16,811,096 B
  (+33,880). The items are captured (exit 0), then the next pass and
  `estate-apply` refuse them `GIT_INVENTORY_MALFORMED`.
- **Walk.** The edge-aggressive median at 119,761 refs and 1,032 tips is
  0.261 s wall and 0.247 s CPU (carry), or 0.135 s and 0.131 s (compact).
  It lists 4 objects and packs 3,342 B. The `--objects-edge` control lists
  4,162 objects and packs 479,876 B.
- **CPU per pass.**
  - Chained capture at 119,761 refs: 54.04 s, against 1.24 s at 256 refs,
    or about 0.44 ms per ref.
  - Grouped pass at 96,849 refs: 1,431.73 s. About 927 s of that is
    projected to the per-head children of `prerequisite_commits`.
- **M inputs** (chained bundle, exact decomposition, check 0):
  - c 1,387 B;
  - a 42 B + refname per ref (73.15 B compact, 173.2 B carry);
  - p 54 B per tip;
  - b 24.6 B per changed-tree entry (1,576 B per 64-entry tree);
  - s 47.0 B raw, 5.7 to 7.1 B packed per seat (filesystem-v1, whole on
    every changed capture);
  - compact bundle 8,845,052 B, 99.05 % of it header refs.
- **Self-test:** 15 tests OK. **check-fast:** green: exit 0, 20:29:14Z to
  20:41:46Z, under the shared flock at nice 10 in `nix develop .#default`.
  gitleaks found no leaks, and all 26 test binaries passed.

  which names corruption rather than size. A pass writes the whole bundle
  before refusing, and `write_source_pack_bytes` stays 0 for it.
  A grouped item's rewritten header is not checked against the cap at
  write, so a capture is recorded that later readers refuse.
  `prerequisite_commits` spawns two git children per base head.
  These are candidates for a v1 follow-up issue. None was filed by this
  lane.
